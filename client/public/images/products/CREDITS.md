# Product image credits

Every image in this folder is a photograph of the keyboard the seeded product
claims to be — not a generic stock shot. All are from Wikimedia Commons under
free licences, downscaled to 800px wide and stripped of EXIF for the demo.

The `CC BY-SA` images require that derivatives keep the same licence. Resizing
is a derivative, so those files remain CC BY-SA; the rest of this repository's
licence does not apply to them.
